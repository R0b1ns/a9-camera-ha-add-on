Add Camera

Connect a camera that is still in its factory access-point mode to your Wi-Fi.

Prepare the camera

  1. 1
    Keep the DNS rewrites in place

    Point *.naxclow.com and *.naxclow.com.cn to the Home Assistant host in your router/AdGuard – otherwise the camera will call the original cloud again.

  2. 2
    Choose your home Wi-Fi

    Use a 2.4 GHz network with a password of 8–63 characters. These cameras do not support 5 GHz or WPA3 – use WPA2 at most.

  3. 3
    Check this Home Assistant

    Checking LAN and Wi-Fi…

  4. 4
    Switch the camera into AP mode

    Power it on, then hold the Mode button for about 5 seconds. It now broadcasts its own Wi-Fi network, usually called NAXCLOW….

Connect the camera

Home Assistant briefly joins the camera's access point, then disconnects again.

Home Assistant joins the camera's AP, sends the settings and switches its Wi-Fi off again. The camera reboots and appears on the overview once it is back online.

Use the local helper

Use a Mac, Windows or Linux computer: join the camera's NAXCLOW… network, run the helper and enter your Wi-Fi there. No installation, no parameters.
